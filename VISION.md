# Vision: Techmyrmidons, reborn as the movement's tool radar

> **Know the tools the best builders actually use. Know where you stand. Know your next step.**

*Status: reimagining proposal, July 2026. The original Techmyrmidons (2018–2021) curated per-domain tool lists, experts, and blogs, and froze when curation ran out of hands. This vision restarts the mission on an engine that cannot freeze the same way.*

## The problem, unchanged since 2018

Technology moves faster than any individual can track. Learners drown in the sea of tools; professionals quietly fall behind; both lack an honest answer to two questions: **what should I learn next in my domain, and where do I actually stand?** The original About page said it well: Techmyrmidons helps you "get wet without drowning."

## What changed: the movement now has a curation engine

The original project froze because a small team curated everything by hand. Today the [Purple Movement](https://github.com/deepusnath/purple-movement-constitution) runs **Evolve chapters**: monthly rooms where practitioners in each domain say, out loud, what they actually build with. That knowledge currently evaporates when the room empties. Techmyrmidons is where it condenses.

**Each Evolve chapter maintains its domain's radar.** AI Evolve owns the AI radar. Space Evolve the spacetech radar. Game Evolve the gaming radar. HR Evolve the HR-tech radar. A radar without a living chapter behind it stays honestly marked as archived rather than pretending to be current. *(Status: proposed to the chapters; each chapter's adoption is its own decision.)*

## The two products

### 1. The Domain Radar

Per domain, a versioned markdown file with four rings, in plain builder language:

- **Adopt**: practitioners here use this in production; learn it with confidence
- **Trial**: serious people are betting on it; good second tool
- **Assess**: watch it, play with it, no career bets yet
- **Hold**: still everywhere, no longer where the future is

Every entry carries a one-line *why* and *who says so* (the session it surfaced in, logged in the chapter's [session logs](https://github.com/deepusnath/Beyond-Gatekeepers)). Updated on a fixed quarterly rhythm per chapter, because rhythm is the movement's core competence. History is kept: the 2018–2021 archives remain as the record of how the landscape moved, and every radar revision is a git commit.

### 2. The Ladder (know where you stand)

Per domain, a four-step self-assessment in plain language: **Explorer → Practitioner → Builder → Myrmidon.** Each step lists observable evidence ("I have shipped X", "I can explain Y to a beginner"), not vibes. Each step links to the next move: a μLearn interest group, a Beyond Borders problem statement, the domain's Evolve chapter. Proof of progress is μLearn Karma, not certificates. The ladder answers "where do I stand" the way the movement answers everything: with proof of work, not marks.

## How it stays honest

- **No pay-to-list, ever.** A vendor cannot buy a ring. Sponsors of anything in the movement get no radar influence (constitutional anti-capture rule).
- **Disagreement is content.** If practitioners split on a tool, the radar says so and links the debate in the session log.
- **Freshness is visible.** Every radar shows its last-updated date and its owning chapter. Stale radars get flagged, not hidden.

## What this is not

Not a news site, not an awards list, not SEO content, and not a rebuild-the-website project. **Content first:** the radars live as markdown in this repo and prove their value raw. A proper site (and retiring the 2018 Bower/Grunt stack) comes only after two chapters have shipped two radar revisions each. The movement does not build websites before it builds habits.

## Where it sits in the federation

Techmyrmidons is the **mastery layer**: the artifact the Evolve network (Beyond Gatekeepers) produces, feeding direction back into Beyond Syllabus (what to learn beyond the curriculum) and Beyond Borders (which tools the problem statements assume). One sentence: **Evolve rooms surface the truth; Techmyrmidons writes it down.**
